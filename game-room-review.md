After the player completes **each of the five room games**, reveal one golden key.

This must work consistently in ALL rooms.

Important:

**Do not automatically collect the key.**

The player must manually click the golden key.

After completing a room:

1. finish the puzzle
2. show the room’s portfolio reveal / completion content
3. reveal a clearly visible golden key
4. wait for the player to click it
5. only then add the key to the inventory
6. mark the room completed
7. update progress from X/5

The key must never be hidden behind another layer or become unclickable.

# 5) PREVENT KEY COLLECTION BUGS

Check every room individually.

Make sure:
- completion state does not cover the key with an invisible overlay
- modal backgrounds do not block the key click
- the key does not appear outside the visible viewport
- the key is not placed behind room content

- collected keys cannot be duplicated
- leaving and returning preserves the collected state
- if the player completes the puzzle but has not clicked the key yet, returning to the room still shows the uncollected key

Do NOT mark a room as fully completed until its golden key has actually been clicked and collected.
